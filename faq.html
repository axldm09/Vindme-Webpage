Account & Email

To change your email address:

  1. 1 Go to Settings in the app
  2. 2 Tap on Account
  3. 3 Select Change Email
  4. 4 Enter your new email address
  5. 5 Check your new email for a verification link
You must verify your new email within 24 hours, or the change will be cancelled.

If you haven't received the verification email, please check the following:

Check your Spam/Junk folder - Our emails sometimes get filtered there
Wait a few minutes - Emails can take up to 5 minutes to arrive
Verify the email address - Make sure you entered it correctly
Add no-reply@vindme.io to your contacts to prevent future emails from going to spam.

If you're unable to verify your new email address, don't worry! Here's what you can do:

Use your old email to log in

Your old email remains active until the new one is verified. Simply log in with your original email and try the email change process again.

  1. 1 Log in with your original email address
  2. 2 Go to Settings → Account → Change Email
  3. 3 Double-check the new email address for typos
  4. 4 Request a new verification email
If you continue to have issues, please contact our support team.

To reset your password:

  1. 1 On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?"
  2. 2 Enter your registered email address
  3. 3 Check your email for the reset link
  4. 4 Click the link and create a new password
For security, password reset links expire after 1 hour.

Privacy & Security

Your privacy is our top priority. We protect your location data with:

  • End-to-end encryption for all location data
  • Secure servers with industry-standard protocols
  • No sharing of your data with third parties
  • Complete control over who can see your location
Yes! You have full control.

You can enable "Ghost Mode" at any time to hide your location from all connections. Go to Settings → Privacy → Ghost Mode.

Ghost Mode can be scheduled to turn off automatically after a set time.

Only people you've explicitly added and approved can see your location. You control:

  • Who can send you connection requests
  • Who can view your real-time location
  • When to hide your location (Ghost Mode)
  • Individual permissions for each connection
